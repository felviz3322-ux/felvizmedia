Privacy Policy

Felviz Media is a desktop content management tool used by the app owner to schedule and publish short-form videos.

The app may store API tokens locally on the user's device only for the purpose of connecting to the user's own TikTok account and publishing videos selected by the user.

The app does not sell personal data. The app does not share user data with third parties.

TikTok authorization is used only to connect the user's TikTok account and publish videos that the user has selected and scheduled inside the application.

The user can remove access by disconnecting the app from their TikTok account or deleting the locally stored credentials.
